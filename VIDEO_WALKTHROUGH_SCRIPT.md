## 🎙️ Word-for-Word Voiceover Script (Balanced 4–5 Lines Per Section)

### 1. Introduction & Problem Statement [0:00 – 0:20]
*(Visual: Homepage, SIH26140 banner, Active Learning Loop)*

> Quantum computing is poised to transform technology, but learning it remains intimidating due to abstract mathematics and a lack of hands-on physical intuition.  
> Traditional textbooks rely on static formulas, while existing tools lack structured pedagogy and real-time cause-and-effect visualization.  
> Addressing **Smart India Hackathon Problem Statement SIH26140**, our team, **Egreen Quanta**, engineered **Quantaneer**: QuantumLearn AI.  
> Quantaneer bridges theory and intuition through an active learning loop: Learn, See, Build, Simulate, Practice, and Master.  
> Built with zero-latency simulation, 3D visualization, and adaptive intelligence, it makes quantum education accessible to every student.

---

### 2. Interactive Circuit Studio & Drag-and-Drop [0:20 – 0:45]
*(Visual: Wire canvas, dragging H and CNOT gates, Undo/Redo `Ctrl+Z`, presets, QASM export)*

> At the center of our platform is the interactive **Circuit Studio**, built with a luxurious, high-contrast light interface.  
> Learners can effortlessly drag and drop single and multi-qubit gate icons onto a rectangular wire window, with full keyboard undo and redo support.  
> Our simulation engine computes the exact two-to-the-n complex statevector amplitudes and measurement statistics in under five milliseconds.  
> Students can instantly load classic algorithm presets like Bell states and Grover's search, or save their custom circuits directly to the database.  
> With a single click, the platform generates production-ready OpenQASM code compatible with IBM Quantum hardware.

---

### 3. 3D Orbital Bloch Sphere & Quantum Tracking [0:45 – 1:05]
*(Visual: Rotating sphere in 3D, coordinate readouts, pure vs. mixed state indicator)*

> To transform abstract linear algebra into physical intuition, Quantaneer features an interactive **3D Orbital Bloch Sphere**.  
> Rendered directly on an HTML5 canvas with smooth mouse controls, it projects single-qubit states alongside exact spherical theta and phi coordinates.  
> For multi-qubit systems, our engine evaluates the subsystem state using partial trace operations to calculate the reduced density matrix.  
> When qubits become entangled, the Bloch vector visibly contracts inward, displaying a purity readout below one point zero.  
> This visual feedback gives students an unmistakable geometric distinction between pure superpositions and mixed entangled states.

---

### 4. Rigorous Curriculum & Guided Roadmap [1:05 – 1:25]
*(Visual: Module cards, KaTeX Dirac notation, inline quizzes, roadmap milestone checkbox)*

> Quantaneer provides a structured **thirteen-stage curriculum** that takes learners from basic qubits to Quantum Machine Learning.  
> Every lesson is typeset with native KaTeX LaTeX rendering, replacing plain text with elegant Dirac bra-ket notation.  
> To guide students step by step, our **Interactive Visual Roadmap** presents a fifteen-milestone journey with direct completion checkboxes.  
> Marking milestones updates their persistent profile in real time, recalculating their overall curriculum progress bar.  
> Every completed stage awards gamified experience points, unlocking authentic milestone badges that celebrate their ongoing learning streak.

---

### 5. Specialized Laboratories (BB84 & Bell CHSH) [1:25 – 1:45]
*(Visual: Bell Lab S=2.828 readout, switching to BB84, toggling Eve intercept, QBER alert)*

> For deeper experimentation, Quantaneer provides five specialized research laboratories.  
> In the **Bell CHSH Lab**, students measure correlations across detector angles, observing an empirical violation value of S equals two point eight two eight.  
> This directly surpasses the classical limit of two, providing unambiguous mathematical proof of quantum non-locality.  
> In the **BB84 Cryptography Lab**, activating Eve's intercept-resend attack collapses quantum states, elevating the error rate to twenty-two percent.  
> Because this exceeds the theoretical threshold, the platform triggers an immediate eavesdropping alert and safely aborts the key.

---

### 6. Practice Hub, Adaptive Arena & Qiskit Sandbox [1:45 – 2:05]
*(Visual: Answering quiz question, difficulty badge shifting to Intermediate/Advanced, confetti, Python editor)*

> Inside the **Practice Hub**, our assessment engine personalizes the learning journey through dynamic AI difficulty scaling.  
> As students answer questions correctly and build answer streaks, the quiz difficulty automatically ramps from Beginner to Intermediate and Advanced.  
> Correct completions reward students with celebratory animations, streak multipliers, and rotating daily circuit construction challenges.  
> For learners advancing into quantum programming, an embedded code editor lets them write and execute standard Python Qiskit scripts.  
> These scripts run directly against our backend simulation engine, bridging visual circuit design with real-world quantum programming.

---

### 7. AI Quantum Mentor & Instructor Copilot [2:05 – 2:30]
*(Visual: AI Tutor conversation, Before/After repair diff, Instructor Dashboard heatmap, remedial generator)*

> When circuits produce unexpected measurement distributions, our context-aware **AI Quantum Mentor** analyzes the circuit structure.  
> It diagnoses phase alignment errors and missing initialization gates, presenting clear 'Before versus After' structural repair comparisons.  
> Powered by Google Gemini two-point-zero Flash with an offline fallback, the mentor ensures intelligent guidance at all times.  
> For teachers, the **Instructor Dashboard** tracks cohort completion percentages and renders class concept difficulty heatmaps.  
> With our AI Instructor Copilot, educators can generate customized remedial review materials and targeted practice assignments in seconds.
